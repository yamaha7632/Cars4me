About Us — Local Car Sales Twin Cities

Tired of overpaying at big dealerships? So are we.

Between high overhead costs, sales commissions, and hidden fees, buying a car from a traditional dealership can feel more like a hassle than a deal. That’s why we set out to do things differently.

Our mission is simple: connect buyers with trustworthy local sellers—without unnecessary fees.

We’ve built strong relationships with a select group of reliable sellers over the years and handpick vehicles that offer great value without compromising on quality or reliability.

Whether you're looking for a dependable daily driver or a budget-friendly option, we’re here to help guide you—not pressure you—through the process. We aim to give you the most bang for your buck and provide honest, upfront details about every vehicle we list.

What sets us apart:

  • No dealership markups or commissions
  • Carefully vetted local sellers in Savage, Burnsville, Shakopee, and Prior Lake
  • Transparent pricing and information
  • Inventory focused on value and reliability

We operate by appointment only, so we can give you the time and attention you deserve. Take a look at our current inventory—we may not have hundreds of cars, but what we do have, we believe in.
